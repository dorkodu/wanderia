🌎✨ Wanderia Idea Validation Report

Decision: GO (with Wedge Pivot)

Confidence Score: 0.85 / 1.0

Idea: Operating System for building an agentic & onchain business (Internet Capital Markets + AI Workforce).

Demand Signals (Score: 2.5/3 - Moderate to Strong)

  • AI Agent Autonomy: Deep market interest in AI agents operating as economic actors (ai16z, Truth Terminal).
  • Internet Capital Markets: Traction on platforms like believe.app, daos.fun, and vvaifu.fun showing willingness from users to fund and speculate on app development and AI narratives.
  • Crypto Necessity: Absolute. You cannot programmatically distribute global, permissionless equity or give AI agents financial custody without blockchains (Solana).

Risks & Feasibility

  • Technical Feasibility: Hard but solvable. A full "OS" is too broad for an MVP. However, building a focused launchpad or treasury primitive using existing tools (Squads, SPL tokens) is achievable in weeks.
  • Regulatory Risk: High. Offering tokenized "equity" touches on unregistered securities laws.
  • Integration vs Build: You do not need to build everything from scratch. Leverage Squads Protocol for the "Programmatic Treasury" and Meteora/Raydium for liquidity.

Recommended Pivot: Wedge Pivot

Don't build the entire "OS" on day one. Build the Agentic Launchpad & Treasury Primitive. Allow a founder to raise funds via a token launch (Internet Capital Market) where funds go directly into a programmatic, milestone-based multisig treasury that can programmatically disburse funds to AI agents for labor.

Next Steps

  1. Define the exact MVP scope (e.g., Fair Launch smart contract + milestone-based treasury).
  2. Audit integration possibilities: Squads Protocol (Treasury) + Pump.fun/Meteora (Launch).
  3. Design the user flow for a founder launching a project.
  4. Proceed to the Build phase.
